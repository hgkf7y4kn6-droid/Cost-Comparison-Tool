Sourcing playbook

Check steps off as you go — progress is saved in this browser.

  1. Download the Department of State registered-businesses dataset from the PA Open Data Portal. Filter County to Lehigh, Northampton, Lancaster and Philadelphia.

  2. Export the L&I Business Licenses / Commercial Activity Licenses dataset from OpenDataPhilly.

  3. Search Open Data NJ for active commercial entities in Camden, Burlington, Mercer and Gloucester counties. Expect entity names and industry tags, not contacts.

  4. Use a rate-limited local-business scraper (Outscraper, PhantomBuster, Scrap.io). Draw polygons around the Route 22 / I-78 Lehigh Valley corridor and Lancaster City. Collect only what the merchant publishes: business phone, generic email (info@, sales@), storefront address.

  5. Greater Philadelphia Chamber, Lehigh Valley Chamber, Lancaster Chamber. Check each site's /robots.txt first and skip any that disallow automated collection.

  6. Consumer emails are purged, duplicates merged, and out-of-territory rows dropped automatically.

  7. Export the list, upload company names/domains to Clay or Apollo.io, and match each company to the regional decision-maker (see title map below). Filter to 20–500 employees and look for intent triggers. Do not scrape individuals from corporate sites. Re-import the enriched file here — rows merge by ID / company.

  8. Run the list through ZeroBounce or NeverBounce before any outreach and record the result on each lead (or include an email_status column on re-import).

  9. Use the draft generator on each lead. Every email carries a clear opt-out line.

Targeting reference

Priority industries, who to contact for each product line, and compliance.

Priority industries

  • Healthcare networks & medical groups — scrubs/uniforms, heavy janitorial, admin furniture (e.g. Penn Medicine, Lehigh Valley Health Network, Penn State Health).
  • Corporate & professional services — workstations, branded apparel, office supplies (Center City financial hubs, Cherry Hill tech, Route 22 corporate parks).
  • Property management & CRE — high-volume janitorial; first to know about tenant move-ins needing furniture.
  • Hospitality, distribution & light manufacturing — durable uniforms, industrial cleaning, warehouse office desks (Lehigh Valley and Lancaster corridors).

Compliance

  • CAN-SPAM permits B2B cold email to business addresses when the offer is relevant to their operations — include your physical address and an honored one-click opt-out.
  • Never email consumer accounts (gmail, yahoo, etc.); they're purged on import and blocked on save.
  • Verify every address before sending to protect domain health.
  • Respect robots.txt and site Terms; prefer government open data and licensed enrichment over scraping company sites.
