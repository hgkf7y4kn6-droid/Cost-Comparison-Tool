Help & support

Email your administrator and include your work email, what you were doing, and what you saw (a screenshot helps). Please don't send passwords.

Common questions

I can't sign in.

Accounts are created by your administrator. After five wrong passwords sign-in pauses for 15 minutes. If you forgot your password, ask your administrator to issue a new one.

The first screen takes a while to load.

The server may be waking up. Wait a minute and try again.

“Customer search isn't connected.”

Your administrator needs to add the Google Places and Apollo keys on the server. Until then you can import lists or add a business from the map.

The map is blank.

The map needs a Google Maps key (set by your administrator) that allows this app's address.

Voice dictation isn't there.

Dictation uses your browser's speech service (Chrome, Edge, Safari on the web). In the phone apps use your keyboard's microphone key.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Account → Delete my account in the app. You can download a copy of your data first from the same screen.

How do I use the app offline?

The app opens and shows what's on your device. Searches, coaching and syncing need a connection and catch up when you're back online.
