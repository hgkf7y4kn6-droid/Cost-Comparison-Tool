Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Sales Hub is a sales app for business development representatives at Office Basics (“we”, “us”). It helps you find business customers, compare costs, build proposals and manage accounts. This policy explains what the app handles, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. Sales Hub is a business tool for adults; it is not directed to children.

Who this covers

Your account is created by your employer's administrator. Your employer decides how the app is used and is responsible for the business data you enter. This policy covers the data the app itself handles for you. Questions go to your administrator.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, we do not sell your data, and we do not collect your precise location. The app asks for the camera (to scan a business card) and, in some browsers, the microphone (for voice dictation) only when you use those buttons.

Who receives data

We use service providers to run specific features. Each receives only what that feature needs:

  • Hosting provider (Render): runs the server and stores the shared data.
  • Google (Maps and Places): when you open the map or search for a business, your device talks to Google, and the customer finder sends the business type and area you choose from our server. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Apollo (apollo.io): when you use “Find buyer”, our server sends a company's website address and receives public business information (headcount, a contact's name and job title). See Apollo's privacy policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude), only if your administrator turned AI on: the text of a note you choose to “Tidy with AI”, a business card photo you scan, or a coach question with a short deal summary (company, segment, stage, city, headcount). Costs, margins and contact details are not sent. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Your browser's speech service (Chrome, Edge, Safari), if you tap Dictate on the web: the audio goes to that browser's provider. The native apps do not offer this button; use your keyboard's microphone key instead.

We may disclose data if the law requires it. If our business is sold, data would transfer to the new owner under this policy.

Business contacts you add

Leads and accounts contain names, business emails, phone numbers and job titles of people at other companies. Use them only for legitimate business outreach: contact business addresses, include a real sender and an opt-out line in every email, and honor opt-out and do-not-contact requests promptly. If one of those people asks to be removed, delete the record and tell your administrator.

How long we keep it

Data stays until you or your administrator delete it. When you delete your account, your sign-in and the server-side records you own are erased immediately, and the app clears this device. Routine server backups, if any, age out on the hosting provider's schedule.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data: in the app, open Account → Delete my account. Or write to your administrator.
  • Get a copy: Account → Download my data, or use the CSV export on each tab.
  • Correct data: edit it in the app.
  • Clear a device: Account → Clear this device removes everything the app stored locally.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, or the EU/UK), you may have further rights to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal data and to complain to a regulator. Contact us and we will respond within the time the law allows.

Security

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are salted and hashed; repeated wrong passwords lock sign-in temporarily. Reps see full details only for the accounts they own; teammates' customer accounts show only the name, address, main phone and spend. API keys for Google, Apollo and Anthropic stay on the server. No system is perfectly secure; tell us right away if you suspect a problem.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, where required, tell you in the app.
